# T05b - Substituent swaps: is there a consistent "add group X" direction?

1. parents = first 300 molecules of data.get_smiles("atoms") that contain an aromatic C-H
   (Chem.MolFromSmiles(s).HasSubstructMatch(Chem.MolFromSmarts("[cH]"))).
2. Substituents X in {"F": "F", "Cl": "Cl", "OH": "O", "NH2": "N", "CH3": "C", "COOH": "C(=O)O",
   "CN": "C#N", "NO2": "[N+](=O)[O-]"}.
   rxn = AllChem.ReactionFromSmarts(f"[cH:1]>>[c:1]{X}"). For each parent take the FIRST product of
   rxn.RunReactants((mol,)) that sanitizes (Chem.SanitizeMol in try/except that records the failure);
   variant = Chem.MolToSmiles(product).
3. Encode all parents in ONE model.run call and all variants of a substituent in ONE call, pretrained
   model, layer 12 and layer 6 CLS. delta = cls(variant) - cls(parent).
4. For each X: consistency = mean of utils.pairwise_cosine(deltas) off-diagonal. Then the 8x8 cosine
   matrix between mean delta vectors. Also do layer 6. Also do it for model.load("random") (control).
results.json: {"n_per_substituent": {...}, "consistency": {kind: {layer: {X: v}}},
               "mean_delta_cosine": {kind: {layer: 8x8 list}}, "order": [X names]}
