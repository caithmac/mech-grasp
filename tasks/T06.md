# T06 - SMILES atom-order sensitivity: where and how much

Verified fact (supervisor): GRASP has ABSOLUTE position embeddings (input position of each atom in the
SMILES string), in addition to graph distances. Reordering the atoms of the same molecule changes the
layer-12 CLS embedding by mean cosine distance ~0.137 (different molecules: ~0.52).

Characterise this. Use 300 molecules from data.get_smiles("atoms"), 5 random orderings each
(Chem.MolToSmiles(mol, doRandom=True, canonical=False)). Encode ALL orderings in ONE model.run call per model.
Use utils.cosine_distance.

1. "per_layer": for layers 0..12, mean/median/max order-distance of CLS AND of mean-pooled atoms
   (acts.mean[l]), pretrained and random-init.
2. "per_atom": order-distance of each ATOM's own vector across orderings, per layer. To align atoms between
   orderings use:
     match = Chem.MolFromSmiles(variant).GetSubstructMatch(Chem.MolFromSmiles(canonical))
   match[i] is the index, in the variant, of canonical atom i. Atom rows of molecule k in acts.atom are
   the rows where acts.mol_index == k, in RDKit atom order. Compare canonical atom i with variant atom
   match[i]. Report the mean per layer.
3. "property_drift": train Ridge(alpha=10) probes on CANONICAL layer-12 CLS of data.get_acts("mols") for
   logp, tpsa, qed (data.get_mol_labels("mols")), then predict those properties for the 300 molecules'
   orderings. Report the mean absolute change in predicted value between orderings of the same molecule,
   and compare to the probe's test MAE. Do the same with layer 6 CLS.
4. "retrieval": for each layer in [3, 6, 9, 12]: % of reordered SMILES whose nearest neighbour (cosine)
   among the 4000 canonical "atoms" embeddings (data.get_acts("atoms","pretrained").cls[l]) is their own
   molecule.
5. "size_dependence": Spearman correlation between a molecule's heavy-atom count and its mean layer-12
   order-distance.
